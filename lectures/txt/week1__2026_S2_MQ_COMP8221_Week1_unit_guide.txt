  • Requirement to Pass this Unit To pass this unit, you must achieve a total
    mark equal to or greater than 50%.
  • Late Assessment Submission Penalty Unless a Special Consideration request




                         raf
    has been submitted and approved, a 5% penalty (of the total possible mark
    of the task) will be applied for each day a written report or presentation

    submitted. The submission time for all uploaded assessments is 11:55 pm. A

    concern.
